# Content Integrity Rules

Flag and require evidence for:
- testimonials and reviews
- customer/user counts
- ratings
- awards
- certifications
- compliance badges
- partner logos
- government affiliations
- security guarantees
- "best", "#1", "guaranteed", or similar objective claims
- statistics
- claims about legal approval

Do not silently create fictional data to make a page appear complete.

For third-party media, verify the project has a license, permission, public-domain status,
or another valid basis before treating it as production-safe.
