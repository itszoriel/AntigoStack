# Data analysis example

## Request

> Analyze the relationship between campaign spend and revenue. Profile data quality, make transformations reproducible, show uncertainty, and do not claim causation unless the study design supports it.

## Expected approach

1. Establish source of truth, grain, time zone, cohort rules, and currency.
2. Quantify missingness, duplicates, outliers, joins, and temporal coverage.
3. Separate descriptive association from causal claims.
4. Choose metrics and visuals that match the decision and failure cost.
5. Validate important totals against an independent baseline.
6. Document privacy constraints, caveats, and reproducible steps.
